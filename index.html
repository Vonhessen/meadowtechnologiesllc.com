Featured Technology

QDIndex

QDIndex investigates the relationship between platform motion and accumulated timing drift. Its prototype processes motion data and a local monotonic clock to produce timing-correction estimates without relying on a continuous GNSS timing signal.

Our next priority is independent evaluation against an authoritative timing reference under controlled GNSS-denied conditions.

QDIndex is an experimental technology. Performance claims require independent validation under representative operating conditions.
